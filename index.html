Before You Start

This is not a medical diagnosis. It is a screening and organization tool that can help an adult describe symptoms, onset, impairment, and overlapping patterns to a qualified health professional.

Answer for the last 6 months unless a question asks about earlier life. If you have learned strategies that hide a difficulty, answer based on the effort required, not only what other people can see.

Questions are intentionally mixed instead of grouped by condition. The report organizes the results after scoring.

Frequency answer buttons include their definitions. For questions that depend on opportunity, answer based on how often it happens when that situation comes up, not only by calendar days.

If you are in immediate danger or might harm yourself or someone else, call emergency services. In the United States and Canada, call or text 988 for crisis support.
